# Cirrava — preliminary U.S. name-clearance screen

**Checked:** July 17, 2026
**Proposed use:** Consumer mobile software for flight tracking and travel information in the United States

## Bottom line

**Do not proceed with Cirrava as the public product name on the present record. It can remain a temporary internal codename, but the safer product decision is to develop alternatives unless U.S. trademark counsel clears the name after investigating the exact-name business and the close airline-software name described below.**

The narrow federal screen found no exact `CIRRAVA` record and no obvious live federal knockout among the prefixes searched. That does not resolve two material marketplace risks:

1. **Cirrava Solutions** is an unrelated California corporation using the exact word, and the project owner has confirmed that neither that business nor `cirrava.com` belongs to this project. Public registration data does not establish whether the corporation and domain registrant are related.
2. **CIRRAVIA** is an active UK software company with documented airline-industry activity—a close visual and phonetic neighbor operating in an adjacent field. [UK Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/10817244) · [IATA airline-retailing program](https://www.iata.org/contentassets/ab84190fc6fb42f19d173e4602a6cd75/air-program.pdf)

## Findings

### 1. Preliminary federal trademark search

A query through the official USPTO Trademark Search backend returned **no federal application or registration for the exact standard-character term `CIRRAVA`**. A broader live-record prefix query for `CIRAV* OR SIRAV*` surfaced five records, none covering software, travel, aviation, or transportation:

- [CIRAVI — SN 90704933 / RN 6729029](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=90704933&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch): bulk-transport sacks, International Class 22.
- [CIRAVOYA — SN 99903603](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99903603&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch): artwork, Class 16.
- [CIRAVEA — SN 99906877](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99906877&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch): furniture, Class 20.
- [SIRAVEXA — SN 99510007](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99510007&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch): adult products, Class 10.
- [SIRAVIC — SN 88490999 / RN 5955369](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=88490999&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch): clothing, Class 25.

This was a **narrow knockout screen**, not a complete USPTO clearance search. The public search UI requires JavaScript, so the query used its official backend; exact individual searches for every phonetic permutation and a full review of coordinated/related classes were not completed. The [USPTO search system](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results) remains the authoritative place to re-run and preserve the final search strategy.

### 2. Exact U.S. company name and domain: Cirrava Solutions

The project owner confirmed that **Cirrava Solutions** and `cirrava.com` are unrelated to this project. Public corporate-record results identify Cirrava Solutions as an active California corporation formed in February 2025 for consulting; the filing should be independently retrieved and preserved from the [California Secretary of State's official Business Search](https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business/) before any legal conclusion.

An entity-name filing by itself does not establish the scope or priority of trademark rights, and the current screen did not verify what services the company actually sells under the name. Nevertheless, another U.S. business's adoption of the **exact name**, together with its control of the exact `.com`, is a material practical conflict and a potential common-law trademark issue. It raises searchability, mistaken-contact, domain, and possible source-confusion problems even if no exact federal registration exists. The USPTO notes that federal search results do not include all unregistered common-law rights and that internet, state, and business-name searches are part of comprehensive clearance. [USPTO comprehensive-clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)

### 3. Close airline/software name: Cirravia

[Companies House lists CIRRAVIA LTD](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/10817244) as an active UK company, incorporated in 2017, with business activities in software development and IT consultancy. An [official IATA event program](https://www.iata.org/contentassets/ab84190fc6fb42f19d173e4602a6cd75/air-program.pdf) identifies a Cirravia co-founder and CTO on a panel about airline retailing and interlining.

This does not establish U.S. trademark rights or prove that the business is currently trading in the United States. It does make `CIRRAVIA` the highest-priority name for counsel to investigate because:

- the difference from `CIRRAVA` is only one letter;
- both can create a similar sound and commercial impression; and
- the documented fields overlap in software and airline/travel technology.

The USPTO explains that marks need not be identical: similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression can matter when the goods or services are related. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)

However, **Šírava Travel** is distributed in the U.S. App Store and Google Play in the travel category. It is a regional tourism/discount app rather than a flight tracker, but `Šírava` is a relevant phonetic neighbor that counsel should include in marketplace and common-law analysis. [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%C5%A1%C3%ADrava-travel/id6498313635) · [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.sirava.travel)

Store-search results are not proof of trademark availability, and listings can change.

### 5. Aviation/geographic use of Cirava

`Cīrava`/`Cirava` is also the name of a Latvian location and airfield. Latvia's official aeronautical information identifies a `CIRAVA` aviation activity area operated by an aviation club. This is not an identified consumer software brand, but it creates additional aviation-context use that a final search should account for. ## Recommendation

**Preferred course:** begin a new naming round and keep `Cirrava` only as a reversible internal codename. Do not publish store listings, acquire permanent brand assets, or file a trademark application under the name until counsel has assessed the exact-name user's rights.

If the team still wants to preserve `Cirrava` as a candidate, commission counsel to:

1. Run a comprehensive federal search for exact, phonetic, visual, and connotation variants, including `CIRRAVA`, `CIRAVA`, `SIRAVA`, `CIRRAVIA`, `CIRAVIA`, `SIRRAVA`, `SIRRAVIA`, and relevant wildcards.
2. Review live and dead records in the likely core classes—downloadable mobile software (Class 9), travel/transport information (Class 39), and hosted software/SaaS (Class 42)—plus coordinated classes and related descriptions.
3. Retrieve Cirrava Solutions' complete California filing, determine its actual first use, services, customer channels, geographic scope, domain use, and any state or common-law rights, and assess whether coexistence is realistic.
4. Investigate CIRRAVIA LTD's current operations, geographic reach, U.S. customers or use, registrations outside the United States, and any enforceable U.S. common-law rights.
5. Search state trademark and corporate-name databases, domain history, app stores, industry directories, social handles, and broader web/common-law use.
6. Advise on registrability, infringement risk, the filing basis and owner, and whether any consent/coexistence or domain arrangement would be needed before an intent-to-use filing and launch.

The USPTO itself says a comprehensive clearance search should include federal records, state sources, domain registries, international databases, and common-law internet use, and recommends using an experienced U.S.-licensed attorney for interpretation. [USPTO comprehensive-clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)

## Scope and limitations

This document is preliminary product research, **not legal advice or a legal opinion**. It does not establish availability, ownership, non-infringement, registrability, or priority. It did not exhaust state databases, unregistered U.S. use, WIPO/EU/UK trademark records, foreign-language variants, corporate registries beyond the noted result, social handles, or paid trademark databases. Negative search results are especially time-sensitive.
