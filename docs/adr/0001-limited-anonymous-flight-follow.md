# Allow one limited anonymous Flight Follow

Cirrava allows an Anonymous User to maintain exactly one Active Flight Follow with cached status and default critical notifications. It becomes a Completed Flight Follow after the server confirms arrival at gate, cancellation, or a completed diversion and performs final reconciliation; the follow slot then becomes reusable, while the completed flight remains read-only in the local cache for 24 hours. An Account Holder is required for additional simultaneous follows, Trips and history, cross-device recovery or synchronization, sharing, and custom notification preferences. When an Anonymous User creates or signs into an account, the existing Flight Follow, notification continuity, and cached timeline transfer automatically to that Account Holder. This reduces first-use friction while containing the identity, abuse-control, and data-merging complexity introduced by anonymous access; anonymous activity remains server-authorized and rate-limited.
