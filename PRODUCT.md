# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cirrava serves people monitoring a scheduled commercial flight that departs from or arrives at a U.S. airport. They may be traveling, meeting someone, or following family and friends; a Flight Follow never assumes they are a passenger. They often check the product while moving, distracted, under time pressure, in poor connectivity, or from a lock screen. Their primary job is to understand what changed, what matters now, and whether the displayed information is fresh.

## Product Purpose

Cirrava makes one upcoming flight dependable to follow from search through its reconciled terminal outcome. Success means a user can identify the correct operating flight, understand its phase and disruptions without decoding provider terminology, receive only meaningful updates, and reopen the last accepted state offline. The public product name remains undecided; Cirrava is the internal codename.

## Brand Personality

Calm, assured, precise. The product should feel attentive without feeling anxious, technically capable without exposing machinery, and premium through restraint and clarity rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

- A generic cross-platform template with interchangeable cards and default components.
- A dense airline operations console that makes consumers interpret raw status codes.
- An urgency-driven travel app that turns every update red, flashes, or over-notifies.
- A copy of Flighty's layouts, language, illustrations, motion, or other distinctive identity; Flighty is a quality benchmark only.
- An airline-branded experience that suggests sponsorship or uses unlicensed logos and liveries.

## Design Principles

1. **Current truth first.** Make the present phase, disruption, time, terminal, gate, and data freshness faster to understand than the supporting detail.
2. **Calm under change.** Treat delays and operational changes with direct language and stable hierarchy; reserve interruption for genuinely important events.
3. **Cached state is real state.** Preserve useful accepted information through weak connectivity and show its age instead of replacing it with an error.
4. **Glanceable by construction.** The open app, notifications, and Live Flight Surfaces share one concise information model.
5. **Identity without imitation.** Build a recognizable Cirrava visual language from original typography, composition, motion, and generic iconography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

V1 targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA and requires manual VoiceOver and TalkBack completion of the core flow. Critical flight meaning cannot depend on color, motion, hearing, precise gestures, or small text. The interface supports system text scaling, reduced motion, platform-sized touch targets, clear semantic announcements, locale-aware time presentation, and direct U.S. English suitable for distracted or stressed users.
